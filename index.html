Privacy Policy for Dividend Retirement Planner

Last updated: 2 July 2026

Dividend Retirement Planner is a macOS app for local retirement and portfolio planning.

Summary

The app is designed to keep your planning data on your Mac. The developer does not operate an account system, analytics system, advertising system, or tracking system for this app.

Data You Enter or Import

The app may process information you choose to enter or import, including:

  • retirement planning assumptions;
  • profile inputs such as age, retirement timing, spending targets, and contribution assumptions;
  • portfolio holdings, security names, tickers, quantities, prices, cost information, dividend/coupon assumptions, and notes;
  • PDF files you choose to import for holdings extraction.

This information is stored locally by the app on your Mac. The developer does not receive this information from the app.

PDF Processing

PDF text extraction, OCR, and holdings-table parsing run locally on your Mac. The app does not upload your selected PDF files or extracted PDF text to the developer.

Market Data Requests

When you search for securities or refresh market data for accepted holdings, the app may connect to public market-data services to retrieve securities, prices, dividends, and Singapore Savings Bond information. These requests may include the relevant ticker, security identifier, or public security name needed to retrieve the requested market data, plus standard technical network information handled by the network provider.

The app does not send your full portfolio PDF, full retirement profile, date of birth, spending assumptions, or contact details to the developer.

Data Collection by the Developer

The developer does not collect personal data from this app.

The app does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, user tracking, or developer-operated user accounts.

Tracking and Advertising

The app does not track you across apps or websites. The app does not show advertising.

Data Sharing or Selling

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share personal data collected from the app, because the developer does not collect personal data from the app.

Local Storage and Deletion

Planning data is stored locally on your Mac. You can delete portfolio records inside the app using the portfolio reset controls. You can also remove the app and its local data using normal macOS app-data removal methods.

Apple Platform Services

Apple may process App Store, purchase, crash, or diagnostic information according to Apple's own privacy policies and your device settings. That processing is separate from this app developer's data collection.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact or support website provided on the App Store product page for Dividend Retirement Planner.
